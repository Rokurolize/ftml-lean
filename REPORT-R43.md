Every function follows the reference's `Text_Wiki_Render_Xhtml_<Rule>::token()` in `ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/<Rule>.php` under the harness configuration of `ref-php/harness/wiki-config.php`. Where a PHP body falls through without returning, PHP concatenates the resulting `null` as the empty string and the mirror returns `[]`.

Two construction notes. Each component was verified to reduce by `rfl` individually at default depth; only the composition needs the raised budget, and the device is disclosed here rather than hidden in the file.

## 7. R4-C2 adjudicated: the Footnoteitem separator is neither Tighten's nor Footnoteitem's

The open question (GAP-LEDGER.md, R4 candidates from the pilot): for Footnoteitem bodies beginning with a block or list element, live's captures `footnoteitem/p-div-first` and `p-list-body` show no space where the reference's Footnoteitem render emits one (live `...</a>.\n<div>` and `...</a>. * one\n<ul>` against the reference's `...</a>. <div...>` and `...</a>. * one<ul...>`); the candidate fix locations were the Footnoteitem render's separator logic and Tighten's newline deletion, the second half of the question being unanswerable until Tighten was in scope. It is in scope now, and the verdict is that the difference lives in **neither**: the reference's separator is the complete Text_Wiki-layer behavior, and live's newline is a post-render block-boundary phenomenon of the serializer / Stage 2 purify layer. The evidence, in the order it settles the question:

1. **Tighten is excluded by construction.** Tighten's render is the constant empty string and it emits no tokens; its parse pass (pipeline position 68, the last) shapes only residual text segments: it deletes raw newlines in text, folds space runs, hoists and ejects Colortext edge spaces, and trims spaces at Paragraph and Newline token edges. A separator emitted at render time by a different rule is not a text segment and does not exist while Tighten runs.
2. **Tighten is excluded by the parse state.** In the current reference's full-pipeline parse state for p-div-first (generated for this adjudication, `conformance-r43/r4c2-states-full.jsonl`), the segment sequence around the item is: Footnoteitem start token, then an **empty** text segment, then the Div start token. There is no text, no newline, and no space between the two tokens for any parse rule to have shaped; the stored body the render consults is the raw string `[[div]]\nblock body\n[[/div]]`, and the Footnoteitem token itself carries only `{type, id}`.
3. **The reference's separator is unconditional on body shape.** The Footnoteitem render (mirrored in R4.1 and proven over the notes corpus) emits `</a>.` plus a single space iff the stored body is non-empty; it never inspects what the body begins with. The current reference tree, re-run for this adjudication through both `batch.php render` and the restricted harness (they agree byte-for-byte), still emits the space on both captures: `1</a>. <div><p>block body</p>\n\n</div>` and `1</a>. * one<ul>\n<li>two</li>\n</ul>`. The mirror, rendering the fed states, is excluded on these two cases only by the unmirrored Div and List tokens, and its Footnoteitem arm is the same proven function, so at the separator position the mirror and the reference agree by the R4.1 proof.
4. **Live's newline tracks rendered block boundaries, not the separator.** Seven fresh probes under prefix `muse-probe-r43-` on sandbox-for-codex (all valid, stable, and source-matched; cases and captures in `conformance-r43/r4c2-probes/`) discriminate the shapes. A one-line div body (`X[[footnote]][[div]]block body[[/div]][[/footnote]]`) and the same body with a newline before the div: the div is not claimed at all (Wikidot's div needs its multi-line form), the body renders as literal text, and live emits the **space**: `1</a>. [[div]]block body[[/div]]`. A newline-framed one-line div body behaves identically. A text-then-div body emits the space. A span-first body in the exact multi-line frame of p-div-first emits the space: `1</a>. <span>inline body</span>`. A heading-first body is not claimed in the footer context and emits the space. And in p-list-body itself, live **keeps** the space after the dot (`. * one`) and the newline appears later, before the `<ul>`, mid-body where no separator exists. So the separator position shows a newline exactly when the body's first rendered node is a claimed block element, and the same newline-before-block appears at a mid-body block boundary: one phenomenon, located at block boundaries in the rendered output, after every render rule has run.
5. **Disposition.** The phenomenon belongs to the post-render serializer / Stage 2 (`purifyHtml`) layer, the same surface family as the List and Paragraph frame differences the R2 records already attribute there, and it is routed to that record as candidate **C-R43-1** for the STAGE2 assessment (whose crossing corpora cover exactly this boundary class). The reference takes no correction at Footnoteitem: correcting its separator to a conditional newline would falsify the Text_Wiki layer it specifies, as the span-first and unclaimed-div probes show the space is live's own separator whenever no block boundary follows. No ftml-side candidate arises: ftml already shows the newline (R2-NOTES finding F2), so on this shape ftml and live agree and the reference is the odd one out only at the Stage 2 surface, which the render tier's Bridge A comparison explicitly does not cover (R4-SCOPE.md). R4-C2 is closed by this adjudication; the entry appended to this worktree's GAP-LEDGER.md copy records it and names C-R43-1.
