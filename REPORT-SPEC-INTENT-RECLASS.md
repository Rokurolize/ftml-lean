## 2. How the three statuses are used

`(a) agrees` means the statement's concrete cases match the current mirror function in substance, by the scratch elaboration or by the mirror source named in the row. `(b) diverges` means they do not. When a correction that was supposed to discharge the old divergence pin has landed, the row says so and says why the statement still does not match. When no such correction applies, the row says not applicable. `(c) intent error` means the statement is one of the seven blockquote and iftags statements already dispositioned in `REPORT-SPECINTENT.md`. The scratch confirms the mirror still deletes and suppresses as live does, so those seven stay intent errors rather than becoming agreements.

The statements are theorems of the intent model inside `SpecIntent.lean`. The only mirror name they mention is `litBytes`. Agreement is therefore agreement of the concrete inputs and outputs with the corresponding mirror functions, not a shared proof term.
