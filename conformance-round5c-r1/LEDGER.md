This ledger was written in full before any live probe. Every suspicion below was raised by fresh static reading of the corrected Xhtml render sources under ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/ (Div, Span, Size, Collapsible, Tabview, List, Blockquote, Clearfloat, Note, Center) and their parse-side companions, plus local reference-harness renders of the same sources (php is not a live probe). The decisive live byte for each row is its CONFIRM condition; the byte that clears it is its CLEAR condition. All rows are graded only after the standard probe norm() mask plus the campaign frame mask, so inter-tag newlines, trailing paragraph newlines, &nbsp;/U+00A0 spelling, random tab ids, date attributes, attribute ordering, token-id counter digits and Stage 2 purifyHtml frame differences are already normalized away and cannot by themselves confirm a divergence. Stage 2 frame differences are CLEARED by rule, never confirmed. Round-5b cleared constructs are re-probed only where a REFCORR9 correction changed their mechanism, and each such row carries an explicit Mechanism reason below.
