## 3. The model: structure validated, five rule corrections

The draft's five-pass structure (pass 1 frame/tag normalization, pass 2 paragraph unwrap, pass 3 repair stack with an istack of deferred inline reopenings, pass 4 whitespace/prune tree pass, pass 5 block joining) is validated as a structure: each pass corresponds to settled transformations, and no pass had to be replaced. The draft's istack mechanism carries T3 and T5 as designed, including, emergently, T3's reassignment bound (the istack holds one entry per tag name, so a closed inner span consumes the shared span entry and the target's later closer takes the stack-pop path; this is consistent with all 61 authored probes, and G2-SCOPE states the observable configuration rather than this mechanism as the rule). Five corrections were forced by battery evidence:

M1, pass 2 unwrap scope (forced by p09 against corpus case in2-x48 and p29). The draft unwrapped any paragraph whose body contained a block trigger, which deleted the paragraph live keeps in p09 (`<p>A<br />\n<div align></p>`, where live closes and keeps the paragraph before the div). The correction unwraps only when the body's block structure is fully balanced or contains at least one balanced block pair beside any unclosed starts (new `unwrapScopeScan`/`unwrapBalanced`). A first stricter form (full balance required) regressed in2-x48, whose body holds balanced pairs beside three unclosed divs and which live does unwrap; battery C caught that, and the pair rule is the fitted boundary between the two pinned shapes (its wider boundary is remainder U2).

M2, pass 2 segment separator (forced by p29). The draft inserted a newline marker after an unwrapped segment, which downstream passes rendered as a space between two unwrapped flow segments; live joins them with nothing (`midtext`). The marker is removed; block-adjacent joins still take their newline from pass 5.

M3, pass 3 multi-element crossings (forced by p12, p13, and q04; p15's first crossing confirms). The draft closed the target and reopened crossed elements in istack order. Live keeps the target open and reopens the crossed non-span elements in pop order, reversing their nesting, and drops crossed spans. Implemented as a new branch for depth at least 2 (`popCrossedForReopen`, a new `pendingReopen` field on the repair state, and `hasDeferred`/`reopenDeferred` extended to flush it in pop order ahead of istack deferrals, so the existing whitespace-hoisting behavior in the text handler applies unchanged). This fixed p12, p13, and q04 outright and p15 up to its unsettled tail.

M4, pass 3 anchor boundary (forced by q10). The draft sent anchor targets down the same deferred-reopen path as every other inline; live closes the crossed element outright and never reopens it when the target is an `a`. The anchor branch now pops crossed elements without deferral at any depth (depth 1 is the evidenced case; the any-depth extension is remainder U4).

M5, pass 4 break dropping (forced by corpus case tab-x16, bounded by tab-x36). A `<br />` directly after a block-level sibling and before another block-level element is dropped (`dropBlockSandwichedBrs`, applied to the child lists in pass 4). A first form that looked past whitespace text nodes also dropped the break in tab-x36, where the break forms its own segment separated from the preceding block by a text node and live keeps it; the final rule requires the break to be the literal next sibling of the block.

Same family as F-R2-51 (live rendering a break plus one literal closer for same-line div openers).

## 7. Unsettled remainder, as the probe shapes that would settle it

U1, the p15 tail (the one battery case that still disagrees). Settling shapes: (i) a depth-at-least-2 crossing that drops a span, followed by the dropped span's own closer and plain text; (ii) the same crossing followed by the target's own closer while a reopened element is still open; (iii) a stray closer for an element closed by an earlier single-crossing rotation, with no dropped span involved, to test whether reopening-at-a-stray-closer exists outside the dropped-span configuration.

Settling shapes: a paragraph body with one balanced div followed by one unclosed div; a body with two unclosed divs and no completed pair; a div-nested (non-root) paragraph whose body holds balanced pairs beside unclosed starts.

U3, the break-drop bounds (T11). Settling shapes: a break directly between div/div, paragraph/table, and blockquote/list sibling pairs; a break as the first or last child of a div between blocks.

U4, the anchor boundary at depth 2 and beyond (M4 implements no-reopen at any depth from the single depth-1 observation q10). Settling shape: an anchor target with two crossed inline elements.

U5, lone-content paragraphs. Settling shapes: a paragraph whose sole content is one image (p29 pins text on both sides of the image, not the lone case) and a paragraph whose sole content is one `hr`.
