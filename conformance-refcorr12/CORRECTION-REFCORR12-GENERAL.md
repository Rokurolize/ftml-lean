The general rule. There is one rule with three facets, applied at the rule's attribute consumer rather than in the shared getAttrs. First, the right-trimmed family is the technical/structural attribute set: in the block/container family (Div, and Span where a value survives) every kept value passes through the Wikidot normalizer that drops C0/DEL control bytes, collapses runs of W = {space 0x20, tab 0x09, LF 0x0A, VT 0x0B, FF 0x0C, CR 0x0D} to one space, removes the leading run and now removes the trailing run; in the Image family the accepted technical values `link`, `width` and `class` are right-trimmed of an entire trailing W run, which generalizes the REFCORR11 link-only trim. Second, the whitespace-keeping family is the text/label set: Image `title` and `alt` (and, conservatively, `style`, `height` and `size`, for which no cached trailing-space case exists) are emitted with authored whitespace verbatim, which is the discriminating boundary of P3E-06, P3E-07 and P3E-10. Third, placeholder retention: an authored attribute value that carried a nested-rule 0xFF token marker keeps its key with an empty emitted value (`k=""`) rather than being dropped; Div already implements this with an authored-nonempty record plus a render clear arm, Span gains the same uniform mechanism for the `data-*` family only, because the cached R5DR1-S1 (`id`) and R5BR1-S1 (`class`) placeholder shapes are byte-clear with the key dropped and must not move.

Why the split is rule-local. ftml's `normalize_wikidot_html_attribute_value` is the block-argument normalizer and its pending-space logic drops the trailing run, so Div's former ltrim-only `normalizeValue` (which rtrimmed only `class` in the caller) was one byte short on `type`, `style`, `role` and `data-*`; making the normalizer itself right-trim fixes the whole family at once and leaves the class caller's extra rtrim harmless. Image is an inline rule whose `link`/`width`/`class` values go through a separate consumer trim while `title`/`alt` are raw text, so a global getAttrs trim would be wrong and would break the title/alt pins. Trimming at the consumer, after the C3 leading-space rejection, the REFCORR3 scheme anchoring and the `*` new-tab strip, keeps every acceptance guard seeing the authored bytes and every prior Image decision intact.
