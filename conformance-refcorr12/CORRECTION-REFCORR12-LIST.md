# CORRECTION-REFCORR12-LIST (draft) — GENERAL nested-list / trailing-prose rule for the raw-case failed-closer adjacency

## General rule (the single coherent generalization)

When a raw-case (any uppercase byte) explicit list opener has no pairing block closer, live emits ONE list whose body is governed by two boundary facts. First, a nested raw-case `[[UL]]`/`[[OL]]` opener or closer is not a nested list on live: it is absorbed as an ITEM BOUNDARY, closing the current item so that the following `[[LI]]` becomes a sibling item in the same flat list; lowercase paired lists are untouched and remain genuinely nested. Second, at the failed block closer the current item absorbs the remaining tail LITERALLY through the surviving final block closer AND through all trailing prose after it; only a `[[footnoteblock]]` present in that trailing material is moved past the list-end token so its collected footnote footer renders outside the list. The failed closer, the redundant `[[/LI]]` before it and the nested boundary tags are consumed, never emitted as structure. This is one rule with two boundary clauses, not two special cases: it extends the REFCORR11 adjacency-tolerant guard (which already tolerated any intervening bytes between the failed closer and the next `[[LI]]`) by defining what the preserved bytes mean when they contain a nested opener or trailing prose.

Formally, for a synthetic raw-case opener body that matches the adjacency guard, let `pre` be the bytes before the first failed closer and `post` the bytes after it. Strip raw-case ul/ol tags from `pre` and split the result at every `[[LI]]` opener into sibling item bodies; the last segment is the absorbing item's leading body. From `post` consume one leading LF (the closer's own line end), rtrim, stop at the last surviving `[[/(ul|ol)]]` closer, and strip any leading run of item/block closers left by a nested flatten. The bytes after that last closer are the trailing material: everything before a `[[footnoteblock]]` marker stays appended to the absorbing item's literal body, while the marker and everything after it are emitted after the list-end token. Token creation order and the four-token list wire (start, item-start, item-end, end) plus Newline tokens are unchanged.

This corrects the two round-5e confirmations. C-R5E-P1-1 and C-R5E-P1-2 (trailing-prose leftover placement, `[[UL]]`/`[[UL_]]` openers): REFCORR11 appended `\nZZZ` after `</ul>`; live keeps `<br />\nZZZ` inside the last `<li>`. C-R5E-S1-1 (nested-list flattening): REFCORR11 kept `[[UL]]...[[/UL]]` as a nested `<ul>` inside the `outer` item; live closes `outer` and makes `in` a sibling. The rule is constrained by every cached list capture from rounds 5..5e: `L1-C1RESBR`, `L2-C1BLANK`, `L1-C1LOWER`, `L2-C1MIXCLOSE`, `L3-C1CROSSTAG`, `L4-MULTILI`, `LA-ABS` and `s04-li-fn-newline-before-failed-close` have no nested opener and no non-footnote trailing prose, so their wire is byte-identical before and after; `s01-li-guard-footnote-gap` and `s04-list-ft-gap-footnoteblock` exercise the hoist by token identity; `s02-li-empty-item-footnote-nested` and every lowercase paired nested list keep their genuine nesting.
