Steer from the programme coordinator (the Muse side chat): a build-hygiene warning from the raw lane's delivery, which applies to every lane that started from a copied or handed-over tree. The raw lane found that its tree's .lake oleans had been copied from the coordinator tree (FormRawRules.trace recorded the coordinator's LEAN_PATH and its pre-REFCORR10 scanner), so its first evaluation silently proved statements about the wrong tree's function, manufactured a decide failure, and produced a sorryAx audit line. After rebuilding the affected modules from its own branch's source, the same checks passed cleanly. Before you trust any evaluation, gate, or axiom audit in your tree: rebuild the mirror modules your proofs unfold from your own branch's source through the wrapper (a full lake build LeanFtml from your tree), and treat any audit line produced before that rebuild as suspect. If your tree was a fresh git clone with no inherited .lake, a single full build from your own source is enough to be safe. This costs one build now and prevents a false delivery later.
