## What remains and why

The policy's per-rule RuleProofBundle asks for preserves_wf, eq_ref and reachable_tokens. This lane proves the bridge-independent components of those obligations for all eight rules: append-only and exact-count token tables, rule/option/order characterizations, scanner fuel sufficiency, and (where the mirror carries document state) the counter law and monotonicity. The remaining components are conditioned on foundation work outside this branch: parseBytes_byteForm_id and byteForm_parseBytes_id are explicitly unproved in F1's TokenStateProof.lean, so unconditional WFState preservation, TokEquiv and ReachableState preservation cannot be asserted without inventing an axiom; eq_ref additionally needs the F2 reference-side applyRuleRef wiring, which the coordinator performs at adoption. Two sub-obligations are lane-local remainders recorded above: the Heading successful-match shape and the Toc match/fuel decomposition. No bounded certificate, generator, cardinality or hash is claimed: none of these eight rules is Class C, and no finite enumeration was used as evidence in this lane.

Verdict: PARTIAL. All eight Class A rules have substantial universal, axiom-clean theorems against their actual mirror functions, but the three RuleProofBundle fields are not fully instantiated because the F1 byte bridge is unproved and F2 reference wiring is outside this branch; two additional match-shape sub-obligations remain unsupported. No claim is made beyond what is proved, and no hole was left in the delivered files.
