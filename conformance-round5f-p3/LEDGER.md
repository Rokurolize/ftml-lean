# LEDGER — R5F-P3 parse-side structural rules, written before any live probe

Ground is the post-REFCORR12 canonical ground after the snapshot-36 publication repair (verified HEAD `ef05346de6c7dbc75ad3ee965cb5634d3cbaafbf`, "Record snapshot-36 publication and proof-base-v1 freeze"; the gate verdict is `conformance-refcorr12/VERDICT.md` and the REFCORR12 mechanism record is `conformance-refcorr12/CORRECTION-REFCORR12-GENERAL.md`). In this family REFCORR12 changed exactly one mechanism: the Image attribute consumer at `ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Image.php` was generalized from the REFCORR11 link-only trailing rtrim to a loop over the technical keys `link`, `width` and `class`, right-trimming an entire run of the rule's ctype_space / PCRE `\s` byte set {space 0x20, tab 0x09, LF 0x0A, VT 0x0B, FF 0x0C, CR 0x0D}, while the text/label keys `title`, `alt`, `style`, `height` and `size` were explicitly left byte-verbatim (`conformance-refcorr12/CORRECTION-R12A-IMAGE.md`, `coord-units/R5E-P3/UNIT.md`). The same cycle changed the block normalizer in Div.php (uniform trailing rtrim after collapse-and-ltrim) and added Span placeholder-key retention; those are R1's family seam and are not re-probed here. The Module, Raw and Tableadv general mechanisms last changed in REFCORR10 and were given a full third-order pass in R5D-P3: Module's inline-nested-opener newline insertion, Raw's `laterQuad` re-association and general `surplusCloser` formula, and Tableadv's generalized folded data-* key class `[a-z0-9._-]`. The REFCORR10 derivation records derive those general formulas from a small union of cached run lengths and key shapes and explicitly leave longer runs and combined key shapes untested, so the rows below take fresh, not verbatim, cells of those changed mechanisms. Each row has a ledger reason tied to a changed mechanism; no previously confirmed-and-fixed shape is re-probed verbatim. Rows P3F-01 to P3F-08 are fresh boundary cells of the REFCORR12 Image technical/text split (uncaptured text keys, byte-set width, run length, empty-after-trim, leading byte, multi-key interaction). Rows P3F-09 and P3F-10 are fresh Tableadv data-* key cells of the REFCORR10 key-class generalization. Rows P3F-11 and P3F-12 are fresh Raw run-length cells of the REFCORR10 surplus-closer formula. Row P3F-13 is a fresh Module inline-nested-opener cell of the REFCORR10 nested-module newline mechanism. Rows P3F-14 to P3F-16 are fresh Include fold cells using three run-owned include targets recorded in `target-map.json`.

Ground rule for disposition: a suspicion is CONFIRMED only when a valid, stable, saved-source-matching live capture disagrees with the reference on a rule-level value after the standard mask and the owned-surface mask. Serving-layer surfaces, owned frame classes, random ids, wdfiles hosts and token-id counter digits are never confirmations; an invalid capture is UNTESTABLE. No suspicion may be added after probing without a new ledger row and its own probe.
