## 1. Method and standing

This lane re-ran WBS package 2.4's Stage 2 three-way comparison on the current canonical tree, after the REFCORR4, TABLEADV-FIX, REFCORR5, COVERAGE-INLINE, MIRROR-FIX and REFCORR6 adoptions moved the reference's Stage 1 outputs. No live probe was run: every case's wikitext source is byte-identical to the source its retained capture was taken against, and the 34 Stage 1 changes are reference corrections rendering that same source, so no capture ceased to correspond. No ftml file was edited, no cargo was run, no repository-local git identity or identity override was set, and the only Lean invocations went through `~/.local/state/wikijump/muse-bhl/tools/lean-build.sh` one at a time, with all scratch files uniquely named under `conformance-tidy-recheck/scratch/`; sibling lanes were observed holding the serial queue, and this lane never bypassed it. All deliverables were written as each part completed.

The oracle and its configuration are the retained ones, not rebuilt or re-tuned: libtidy 5.8.0 linked directly against `libtidy.so.58`, the XHTML 1.0 Strict wrapper from `WikiTransformation.php`, `indent=false`, `output-xhtml=yes`, `wrap=0`, `preserve-entities=yes`, `tidyParseBuffer` plus two `tidySaveBuffer` calls and no `tidyCleanAndRepair`, with PHP's delimiter/`<body>` extraction. Before using it, the driver was validated by re-running all 266 cases on their old Stage 1 inputs: its outputs matched the retained `model` field on 266/266 cases after whitespace stripping, recorded in `conformance-tidy-recheck/driver-validation.jsonl` and `driver-validation-summary.json`, so the new model numbers are the retained model's own outputs on the new inputs and not a driver artefact.

## 3. Every non-agreeing case classified

The recheck uses the original classification scheme and its exact decision precedence: agreement, harness frame, oracle-configuration gap, version-class residue, model gap, with the same script/id mask and the same recorded case sets for the frame subclasses. The 15 non-agreeing cases are the 6 script/harness-id frames `con-x21-div-in-coll-in-tab`, `con-x22-coll-in-tab`, `con-x23-div-in-tab`, `con-x25-tab-in-coll-in-div`, `con-x29-footnote-in-tab` and `lst-x08-list-in-tab`; the 3 dynamic bibliography-id frames `lst-x13-deflist-in-bib`, `lst-x14-deflist-multi-bib` and `lst-x15-deflist-bib-colon`; and the 6 recorded frame-compensation cases `p29-unwrap-image-table-dl`, `in2-x48`, `lst-x27-deflist-heading`, `tab-x06-table-in-quote-head`, `tab-x16-two-adv-adjacent` and `tab-x44-malformed-adv-opener`. `p34-div-tag-attr-whitespace` and `con-x24-three-nested-divs`, the two recorded Stage 1 candidates in the original pass, are no longer classified: their corrected Stage 1 now yields oracle, model and live byte-identically, which is why the harness-frame count drops from 17 to 15.

For the 6 script cases the model and oracle first differ inside the script frame, for example at byte 94 of `con-x21-div-in-coll-in-tab` where the model has `</script>\n<script` and the oracle has `</script> \n<script`; after applying the scheme's own mask of script blocks and tabview/harness identifiers, model, oracle and live are byte-equal on all six, and the first divergence against live is likewise the local two-script/CDN-script and sequential/hash-suffixed id material. The literal model-gap predicate (`model != live` and `model != oracle`) therefore fires on these six, but the original scheme assigns root-cause precedence to harness frame when the difference is confined to that material, exactly as the original pass recorded for the same six; they are not Stage 2 model gaps, and this lane reports the predicate firing explicitly rather than hiding it. For the 3 bibliography cases the oracle equals the model and both differ from live only in the random hex suffix of `bibcite-1`/`bibcite-2`; masking the suffix makes all three equal. For the 6 frame-compensation cases the model equals live and the oracle differs at the recorded T6 paragraph unwrap or T11 sandwiched-break drop, for example `p29-unwrap-image-table-dl` where the model serves bare `lead <img …` and the oracle keeps `<p>lead <img …`, and `tab-x16-two-adv-adjacent` where the model drops the break between adjacent tables and the oracle keeps `<br />`; these are the empirical compensations recorded in REPORT-STAGE2.md, not tidy behavior and not model gaps. The per-case `model_vs_oracle_first_diff`, `model_vs_live_first_diff`, `oracle_vs_live_first_diff`, equality flags and mask-equality flags are in `conformance-tidy-recheck/recheck-audit.json` for all 15 cases.

No case is an oracle-configuration gap: the retained entity-preserving driver agrees with live on 251/266, and the two cases it gained on this ground are the corrected Stage 1 candidates. No case is a version-class residue: no shape shows the 5.4-versus-5.8 mechanism. No case is a model gap on Stage 2-relevant bytes: on all 15 non-agreeing cases the model either equals live (6 frame-compensation) or differs from live only in masked harness/dynamic material where the oracle differs too (9 script/bibcite cases). The classifier records the explicit predicate for every case, and `classification-summary.json` reports empty model-gap and unclassified lists.
