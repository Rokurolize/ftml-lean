Mechanism. REFCORR7 strips empty-Raw placeholder tokens (`\xFF<id>\xFF` naming `['Raw', ['text' => '']]`) from the already-trimmed body but does not re-trim the whitespace remainder, so two placeholders separated by a space or tab leave a stray interior/edge space that makes the body non-empty and the separator fire. The minimal fix re-trims after the strip. `Parse/Default/Footnote.php::process` adds `$content = trim($content);` immediately after the REFCORR7 `preg_replace_callback`, before storing `vars['footnotes'][id-1]`; the existing `$body !== ''` test in `Render/Xhtml/Footnoteitem.php` then sees live emptiness with no render edit. `Parse/Default/Bibitem.php::process` adds `$visible = trim($visible);` after its strip and emits the re-trimmed `$content = $visible;` as the body; the stored `vars['bibitems'][$label]` is assigned earlier and stays byte-identical, so compared state is untouched and only the pre-existing `empty` flag value and emitted body change. Rejected alternatives: (a) re-trimming only `$visible` for the flag while keeping the emitted body verbatim leaves a one-space `1. ` tail (measured) and does not match live; (b) re-trimming `$content` before the strip is a no-op because the placeholder bytes are non-whitespace; (c) editing the Xhtml render rules is out of the named owner set and would not fix the stored-body state; (d) re-running the original pre-trim after adding a second placeholder is exactly the missing step, not a replacement. The same two edits are mirrored in `LeanFtml/NotesRules.lean` (`footnoteProcess`, `bibitemProcess`) with byte-identical `.before-refcorr8` copies and nine closed `rfl` pins elaborated through the serial wrapper (evidence/MIRROR-REFCORR8.md).
