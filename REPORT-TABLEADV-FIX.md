## Charge and starting state

The two parse states differ on the 74-byte witness `[[table]][[row]][[cell]][[table"]][[/table]]"]][[/cell]][[/row]][[/table]]` and thirteen sibling frames; PHP 8.5.4 agrees with the enumeration. The lane's grammar comparison covered 185,662 strings: enumeration and PHP agreed on all, the first-parse search diverged on exactly the fourteen frames. The checkout had no `.lake` build directory; the local toolchains are Lean 4.36.0 at `~/.elan/bin` and PHP 8.5.4 locally. The R3.10 scripts' WSL endpoint (`~/workspace/native-stack/bin/wsl-ssh.sh`) and canonical corpus (`~/workspace/ftml-lean-equivalence`) are absent, so all PHP oracles were regenerated locally from this tree's unchanged `ref-php` harnesses.

No refined shortcut was substituted: the enumeration is the mirror, which is the shape the brief calls cleanest and the only one for which conformance to the reference is established without an unproved assumption.

## Cost of the repaired search

The enumeration tries every successful parse of a nested match, so a body holding k closed sibling tables has 2^k parses ending at later closers; the mirror now pays that cost rather than silently changing the answer. The fuel bound limits a single path, not the number of paths. `python3 conformance-tableadv-proof/cost_fix.py` timed the repaired mirror driver and a legacy-scanner driver with identical I/O on three corpora, three runs each, minimum reported in `conformance-tableadv-proof/cost-fix-measurements.txt`: R3.10 stage 185 cases 0.057 s versus 0.031 s (1.81x); witness family 14 frames 0.021 s versus 0.021 s (1.00x); full grammar 185,662 strings 8.064 s versus 3.945 s (2.04x). The repaired search is materially slower in bulk (roughly twice the legacy wall time on the large grammar corpus), which is stated here rather than traded for an unproved shortcut; the absolute stage-corpus cost remains tens of milliseconds and no gate timed out.
