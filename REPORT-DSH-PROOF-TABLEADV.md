## What is now proved or certified

The repaired `TableadvProof.lean` keeps the universal head-run laws: extra fuel at or above the source length does not change `advHeadRuns`; a plain (non-quote, non-bracket) byte prefix extends every run; a closed quoted run is taken whole in priority order; and any well-formed chunk head followed by `]]` is the first run returned by `tableadvEnumMatchFirst`. The REFCORR8 lowercase fold and the REFCORR10 `[a-z0-9._-]` data-key class are universally quantified theorems of `advAttrStep`, and the quote-trick witness is a whole parse state of `tableadvScanGo`.

The reachable parametricity statement is already in the kept universal laws: `advHeadRuns_plain_prefix` quantifies over any byte sequence in the plain class (neither quote nor bracket) and `advHeadRuns_quote_front` over any quote-free quoted interior, so the first head's shape depends only on the three observable byte classes, not on the byte values. A separate full class-map theorem (`src.map quote/bracket/plain` commuting with `advHeadRuns`) was attempted as a parallel unit but left unresolved Lean goals in its `qHeadStep_map` induction; it was dropped rather than committed with open goals. This does not weaken the bounded certificate, whose alphabet is exactly those three representatives, and the universal chunk laws cover the class-parametric head identities. The recursive `(?R)` body search remains the named remainder.

The bounded certificate is in `lean-ftml/LeanFtml/TableadvCertificate.lean`: generator `quotUpTo 6` over the three-class alphabet, cardinality 1,093 (`generator_card`), length bound L=6 with interaction depth d=1 for a single head group (explicitly not the policy's d=3 recursive bound), deterministic FNV-1a-32 hash 2479005724 = 0x93C2A01C (`generatorHash_eq`), and `certificate_true` proving by kernel reduction that all 1,093 representative strings pass `headGroupOk`. That predicate checks the mirror's own `advHeadRuns`/`advHeadRuns.go` only, with four structural sanity conditions plus discriminating semantic pins (`bracketStopOk`, `quotePriorityOk`, `plainFirstOk`); independent simulation confirmed it fails on a priority-swapped matcher (122 of 301 closed-quote cases) and on a bracket-as-plain matcher (all 1,093 cases), so the check is not vacuous. The header names strings above length 6, recursive nested-table search and PcreCore equivalence as the remainder; the raw-source predicate is not claimed to lift automatically through the quotient theorem.

The prior PHP-differential empirical bound is recorded here with its generator and hashes, without re-running PHP: generator `conformance-tableadv-proof/grammar.py` (SHA-256 `37b409b2536eabf4e17021d42903999d4034f165d0d01834e2461e25afd7b55f`), 20-symbol alphabet, free language to length 3 (8,421 strings) plus the quote-trick frame language with two-symbol fillers (421² = 177,241 strings), cardinality 185,662 distinct strings, canonical dump SHA-256 `38b233bbfdd272dd932bb0f5b7f2f07803ba79d69ea3a48395080dff73ce77b8` (15,572,070 bytes), zero mismatches for enumeration vs PHP 8.5.4 from `conformance-tableadv-proof/fuzz-fix-comparison.txt`. That corpus is prior evidence for the fixed quote-trick family, not a re-run and not a universal claim.

## Repair round after independent gate check

The independent gate found that the single 885-line `TableadvProof.lean` could not elaborate to completion from a clean extraction: repeated wrapper invocations were killed during its heavy `decide` work before emitting any Lean error, so its six headline theorems were unverifiable as delivered, even though the certificate half verified fully. Kernel OOM records later identified the exact culprit: one concrete `buildAttrsAdv " data-Foo=\"1\" data-foo=\"2\"" "table"` ground reduction exhausted the wrapper scope's 22 GB cgroup even in isolation. The repair splits the monolith into budgeted family modules — `TableadvHeadProof` (head-run laws through `enum_match_chunks`), `TableadvCellProof` (one-cell scan through `tableadvScanGo_chunks`), `TableadvAttrProof` (REFCORR8/10 universal lemmas), `TableadvChunkDefsProof`, `TableadvChunkOkProof`, `TableadvChunkBytesProof`, `TableadvAttrsGroundProof`, `TableadvScanHeadProof`, `TableadvScanWitnessProof` and the `TableadvScanProof` umbrella — with `TableadvProof.lean` now a thin importing umbrella that prints axioms for the six headline theorems. All headline and universal statements are unchanged, and the proof-local `set_option` limits on the large `decide` instance were retained. The twelve concrete `attrs*` kernel pins and the twelve ground-only `scan_*` corollaries that consumed them were not kept as theorems: their values are recorded as VM-evaluated bounded evidence in `TableadvAttrsGroundProof.lean` and `TableadvCertificate.lean`, while the generic `scan_of_chunks` law, the universal attribute lemmas and the full quote-trick witness theorems remain proved. A cheap empty-head attribute pin is used by `witness_process`. Clean-extraction evidence: `git archive 19b6d01` with `.lake` removed, then `lean-build.sh <tree>/lean-ftml lake build LeanFtml.TableadvProof LeanFtml.TableadvCertificate` exited 0 on the first invocation, building all 51 jobs including every new proof module and producing all oleans from that extraction; a fresh wrapper-run `#print axioms` of the six headline theorems exited 0 with only `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.
