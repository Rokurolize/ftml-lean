## What is now proved or certified

The repaired `TableadvProof.lean` keeps the universal head-run laws: extra fuel at or above the source length does not change `advHeadRuns`; a plain (non-quote, non-bracket) byte prefix extends every run; a closed quoted run is taken whole in priority order; and any well-formed chunk head followed by `]]` is the first run returned by `tableadvEnumMatchFirst`. The REFCORR8 lowercase fold and the REFCORR10 `[a-z0-9._-]` data-key class are universally quantified theorems of `advAttrStep`, and the quote-trick witness is a whole parse state of `tableadvScanGo`.

The reachable parametricity statement is already in the kept universal laws: `advHeadRuns_plain_prefix` quantifies over any byte sequence in the plain class (neither quote nor bracket) and `advHeadRuns_quote_front` over any quote-free quoted interior, so the first head's shape depends only on the three observable byte classes, not on the byte values. A separate full class-map theorem (`src.map quote/bracket/plain` commuting with `advHeadRuns`) was attempted as a parallel unit but left unresolved Lean goals in its `qHeadStep_map` induction; it was dropped rather than committed with open goals. This does not weaken the bounded certificate, whose alphabet is exactly those three representatives, and the universal chunk laws cover the class-parametric head identities. The recursive `(?R)` body search remains the named remainder.

The bounded certificate is in `lean-ftml/LeanFtml/TableadvCertificate.lean`: generator `quotUpTo 6` over the three-class alphabet, cardinality 1,093 (`generator_card`), length bound L=6 with interaction depth d=1 for a single head group (explicitly not the policy's d=3 recursive bound), deterministic FNV-1a-32 hash 2479005724 = 0x93C2A01C (`generatorHash_eq`), and `certificate_true` proving by kernel reduction that all 1,093 representative strings pass `headGroupOk`. The header names strings above length 6, recursive nested-table search and PcreCore equivalence as the remainder; the raw-source predicate is not claimed to lift automatically through the quotient theorem.
