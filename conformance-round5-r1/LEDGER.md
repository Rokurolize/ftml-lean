# R5-R1 pre-probe ledger — RENDER-SIDE BLOCK AND CONTAINER RULES

Canonical base e53e7234. This ledger was written in full before any live probe. Every suspicion below was raised by static reading of the Xhtml render sources under ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/ (Div, Span, Size, Collapsible, Tabview, List, Blockquote, Clearfloat, Note, Center) and their parse-side companions, plus local reference-harness renders of the same sources (php is not a live probe). Live Wikidot behavior is definitional however malformed. Evidence order: ftml corpus and integration pins, frozen parity captures, then fresh live sandbox probes fetched once and cached. The decisive live byte for each row is its CONFIRM condition; the byte that clears it is its CLEAR condition. All rows are graded only after the standard probe norm() mask plus the campaign frame mask, so inter-tag newlines, trailing paragraph newlines, &nbsp;/U+00A0 spelling, random tab ids, date attributes, attribute ordering, token-id counter digits and Stage 2 purifyHtml frame differences are already normalized away and cannot by themselves confirm a divergence. No suspicion may be added after probing without its own new ledger row and probe.
