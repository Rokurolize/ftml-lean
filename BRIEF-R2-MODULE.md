# Brief: R2 Modulepre, Module, and Module654 stage as a TEAM lane (DSH)

You are the root session of a team lane in the ftml Lean equivalence loop's reference-PHP workstream (R2). The loop is building a PHP reference implementation that reproduces live Wikidot behavior: the fossil Text_Wiki snapshot, behavior-corrected rule by rule against live evidence (the fossil is a skeleton, never evidence; live behavior wins every disagreement). Earlier stages (Include, the frame rules, Code, Form/Raw/Rawold) are complete and published as snapshots of the public repo; their catalogs are conformance-refphp/R2-INCLUDE.md, R2-FRAME.md, R2-CODE.md, and R2-FORM-RAW.md in this worktree, and they are the shape to imitate. This stage covers the three module rules, eighth to tenth in pipeline order: Modulepre, Module, Module654. This codebase is yours: what it's for and how it's built are your call, and you'll keep building on it.

This stage runs as a team, and the team structure is part of the assignment. As root, your first actions are: create three tasks on the shared task board, one per rule (Modulepre, Module, Module654), spawn one teammate per task, and give each teammate the per-rule brief below scoped to its rule. Teammates work in parallel in this same worktree. You own coordination, the shared infrastructure, the cross-rule interactions, and the integrated catalog; you do not do a teammate's rule work yourself except where a rule is left unfinished and you say so in the catalog. Write prose in files as flowing paragraphs on single lines, never hard-wrapped at a fixed column.
