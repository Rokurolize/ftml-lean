# Gap Ledger — the unproven set (backlog + exhaustion criterion)

Status key: OPEN · IN-PROGRESS · PROVEN (Lean, lake build green, no sorry, axioms ≤ propext) · DISPOSITIONED (recorded evidence-based reason it will not be proven, e.g. semantics belongs to deepwell evaluation, not parsing). A family leaves the ledger only as PROVEN or DISPOSITIONED.

Ordering is by expected proof leverage: small self-contained machines whose equivalence licenses large rule families first, mirroring how the incidents actually clustered. This is the proposed hunt order; families are hunted exhaustively within themselves (ARTEX-style: one entry per hypothesis, plus a standing "no further gap in this family" exhaustion entry that stays open until the family is PROVEN).
