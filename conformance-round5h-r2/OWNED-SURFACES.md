# OWNED-SURFACES R5H-R2 — masks applied before any verdict

Standing scope decisions from COORD-UNITS/PROTOCOL-R5H.md §9 and PROTOCOL-R5.md §45 are applied without re-litigation: the four-adjacent U+FFFD digit class is OWNED, the parity Email index family is closed with zero confirmations and is not reopened, token-counter digits are OWNED, save-normalized sources are UNTESTABLE, and Stage 2 and serving surfaces are CLEARED. No such construct is registered as a rule-level suspicion in LEDGER.md, and none can become a confirmation in this unit.

Mask 1, campaign frame. Both sides are passed through `probe.norm` imported from the standard wikidot-probe tool, which masks per-render random element ids and date `data-delta` / `data-timestamp` attributes and normalises the lightbox script. compare.py additionally collapses inter-tag whitespace (`>\\s+<` to `><`), rewrites `<br />` to `<br>` and `<div >` to `<div>`, and strips leading and trailing frame whitespace. These are the campaign frame mask of PROTOCOL-R5.md step 5 and never hide a rule-level href, label, attribute or scheme.

Mask 2, gallery serving wrappers. Live gallery markup carries a random `gallery-box-<digits>` id, a `with-lb` anchor class, a `gallery-image-size-*` image class and wdfiles-host attachment `src` attributes. These are serving-layer surfaces already cleared in prior rounds (REPORT-ROUND5G-R2 OWNED-SURFACES.md and its predecessors). For case ids containing `-gal-` only, compare.py removes that id, that anchor class, that image class and rewrites `src="..."` to `src="X"`. The rule-level `href` under test in the five Gallery suspicions is left completely intact, so a scheme-map divergence remains visible after the mask.

Mask 3, Stage 2 tidy frame. The canonical reference harness wraps a bare `<div class="error-block">` in `<p>...</p>` and emits an empty `<p></p>` for an empty token render, while live Stage 2 hoists the div and drops empty paragraphs. This is an owned Stage 2 frame difference settled in REPORT-ROUND5B-R2 and REPORT-ROUND5B-S1 and is masked in classify.py. It never changes whether an embed error block, an empty render or a served iframe is the rule-level outcome; that outcome survives the mask and is adjudicated as CLEARED or CONFIRMED on its own bytes.

No other mask is applied. In particular the bytewise percent-encoded hrefs, the authored anchor text, the `target` attribute, the img `src`, embed event or scheme decisions and gallery scheme-map hrefs are compared verbatim after the three masks above. Every DIFF surviving these masks is a candidate rule-level divergence; every DIFF eliminated by masks 1 to 3 is recorded as CLEARED on an owned surface only.
