## What is now proved or certified

The headline theorem notes_title_never_bool_false is universal over every attribute group both notes block parsers can see: titleOpt g is an absent key (OptVal.null) or textVal v (OptVal.str or OptVal.bytes), so it can never be OptVal.bool false. notes_title_register_3_3_closed combines that with CoverageNotes.titleOpt_class_agrees to show that on every parser-reachable title the PHP trichotomy and the mirror emptiness test name the same branch; footnoteblock_start_title_reachable ties the title option to the start token the block parser actually appends. Register 3.3 is therefore closed in the mirror, not merely recorded as an unreachable token-level finding.

The footnote and footnoteblock render laws are the strongest forms available without the F1/F2/F3 kernels: renderFootnote_template and renderFootnote_process_id give the Footnote template as a function of the claimed id only and evaluate it on the exact token shape footnoteProcess appends; renderFootnoteblock_empty, renderFootnoteblock_end and renderFootnoteblock_start_title give the empty-registry domination, the closer, and the full start-token title trichotomy (null defaults to Footnotes, empty authored title omits the title div, other titles are escaped verbatim). The two footnoteitem separator laws resolve the space after the start token from footnoteLeadVisible. footnote_registry_monotone, footnoteProcess_footnotes, footnoteProcess_placeholder and footnoteProcess_token give the registry append and id invariants; the retained control-purity, token-aware emptiness and separator-class facts cover REFCORR10 and REFCORR11 behavior. The bibliography emission law ties the emitted body to bibVisible and the stored entry to bibNorm.

The bounded certificate x_lf_suffix_class enumerates all 256 possible trailing bytes with generator List.range 256, cardinality 256, and reducer decide; it is recorded as a bounded diagnostic, not as a universal theorem. Its statement classifies the mirror/reference disagreement as exactly the shield class. The final hash of the certificate source is recorded in the verification section below.

## Minimal divergence witness and the stopped line

The one surviving mirror/reference divergence is retained as a minimal witness, esc_shield_diverges: for the body bytes X (88), LF (10), ESC (27), the mirror's footnoteStored stores litBytes "X" because the ESC is a shield byte dropped by the unconditional C0 filter before the break decision, while the reference break model refStored keeps [88, 10]. This is a genuine rule-level divergence over the reference regex /[^\n]\n(?!\n)/, not an owned Stage 2 surface. The line is stopped here as the brief requires; a fix belongs to the coordinator's correction round. The related C-R5E-R3-1 confirmation (REFCORR11's C0 filter running before the single-authored-break predicate) is the REFCORR12 candidate in conformance-round5e-r3/REPORT-ROUND5E-R3.md and is the likely correction that will move this witness.

## Likely REFCORR12 rebase points

Any correction that changes the order of the C0 strip and hasSingleBreakByte in footnoteProcess, or changes the dropped-byte class, will move footnotePure, footnoteStored, the stored-body branch laws, the control-purity facts, footnoteLeadVisible, the two footnoteitem separator laws, the ESC/shield witness and the 256-byte certificate; these are the primary rebase points.
