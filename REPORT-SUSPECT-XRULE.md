# REPORT-SUSPECT-XRULE — suspicion hunt across the seams of the 68-rule pipeline (2026-11-22)

Lane: BRIEF-SUSPECT-XRULE, executed 2026-11-22 in this tree. Territory: not one rule family but the interactions among all sixty-eight parse rules and their render tier — the class whose settled example is x44, a Url token claimed inside what became an Interwiki label and leaking its placeholder into the render. The brief names BRIEF-SUSPECT-NOTES as the method reference, but no such file exists in this tree; I followed the identical method documented in PROMPT-ANTIGRAVITY-SUSPECT.md, BRIEF-X44.md and the sibling SUSPECT reports: record two differing observable predictions for each suspicion before probing, discriminate with the smallest saved-page probe, fetch once and cache, and give CONFIRMED, DIVERGENCE (candidate record with full evidence; in-tree reference correction allowed with a byte-identical before-copy) or UNREACHABLE (parse-side argument). Suspicion generation came from a close read of the whole PHP pipeline and of the mirror modules, assisted by three parallel read-only scans of the early, middle and inline/render segments; every verdict below rests on fresh live probes made in this lane. Live Wikidot is definitional; no ftml file was edited, no cargo was run, no git identity variable was set, and no commit was made. All artifacts are under conformance-suspect-xrule/; the probe ledger with the pre-probe predictions is LEDGER-PREDICTIONS.md, the byte comparator is compare.py, the full candidate and correction records are CANDIDATES.md, and the before-copies are in before/.

## Method, evidence and reachability policy

Thirty-seven probe pages were created with scripts/wikidot-python scripts/wikidot-probe create on sandbox-for-codex under the permanent prefix muse-probe-suspect-xrule- and are left in place; all thirty-seven captures (captures-b12.json, captures-b34.json, captures-b5.json) are valid=true, stable=true and saved_source_matches=true. The reference side was run on PHP 8.5.4 through this tree's ref-php/harness/batch.php (battery regressions) and transform.php (byte-exact single cases); comparison applies only the programme's frame-level shaping — CRLF to LF, <br /> to <br>, collapse of newline runs, outer-newline strip — so entities, spans, labels, @ runs and leaked digits survive verbatim. Two suspected source shapes carrying whitespace-only lines failed the probe tool's read-back verification (live normalizes them on save) and are reported as untestable rather than used as evidence. Reachability was scored before probing: shapes a real page could contain (a code sample with blank lines, a raw span inside a link target or label, a doubled umlaut in prose or a heading, a code example inside a form or module) outrank the adversarial-only shapes (a triple link inside a named anchor or user name, a described email nested in inline math); the distinction is stated on every ledger row.

## Unreachable and untestable surfaces

The Delimiter rule's search for an authored 0xFF byte cannot be driven through any combination of the seams in this brief: every saved source round-tripped as valid UTF-8, and a literal 0xFF cannot be authored or stored, so the proposed `@@A<FF>B@@` route is UNREACHABLE on the parse side — the only way the byte enters the stream is the render-side U+FFFD conversion settled in REPORT-UFFFD.md, which is a different item. The Freelink cross-site `site` option, Url's `post_vars` branch and the Fossil-only `üü` Prefilter strip are likewise unreachable or already removed and were not re-probed. X5, X14 and X29 are the three untestable items with the reasons recorded above, and X13 was dropped before probing for lack of two observable predictions. The static readings also found a family of adversarial-only placeholder swallows (Gallery source lines, Button text attributes, Embed bodies, User names and Toc/Footnoteblock/Bibliography titles holding a Freelink or eref token); X28, X30 and X33 probe representative members, and the rest are the same option-embedding mechanism, not separate verdicts.

## Divergences, corrections and mirror consequences

Five divergences were corrected in this tree, each with a byte-identical before-copy in conformance-suspect-xrule/before/ and a full record in CANDIDATES.md. C-SUSPECT-XRULE-1 adds a delimiter guard to Freelink::process, C-SUSPECT-XRULE-2 strips earlier-rule placeholders from Form body lines, C-SUSPECT-XRULE-3 stops Module from consuming a body (and its closer) that contains an earlier token, C-SUSPECT-XRULE-4 changes the render-time Toc sentinel from the authorable `üü` pair to an unforgeable NUL pair in Text/Wiki.php and Render/Xhtml/Toc.php, and C-SUSPECT-XRULE-6 leaves a named-anchor match literal when its name contains an earlier token. Every corrected decisive probe now matches live byte-for-byte; the regression renders are byte-identical before and after on the 60-case Freelink battery, 68-case Form/Raw battery, 52-case Module battery, 56-case Toc battery and 60-case Anchor battery (regress-*-before.jsonl / -after.jsonl in the artifact directory). C-SUSPECT-XRULE-5 (Mathinline's one-level resolution) is the one divergence deliberately left uncorrected: live restores the entire authored body rather than recursively substituting the inner Email token, and the observed bytes do not justify a local edit to either Mathinline or the described-Email claim without a dedicated battery. The mirror consequences are text for the coordinator's adoption pass: freelinkProcess in LinkRules.lean, formProcess in FormRawRules.lean, moduleMatch/modulePasses in ModuleRules.lean, namedAnchorScan in FinalRules.lean, and tocStripDelim/postStripToc in RenderCore.lean each transcribe the pre-correction behavior and now disagree with the corrected reference; none was edited here.
