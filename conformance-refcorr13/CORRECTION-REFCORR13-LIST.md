## The three minimal symmetric rules

R13-S1 (nested-boundary case symmetry, fixes C-R5F-P1-1, C-R5F-P1-6, and the identically-shaped cached `s16-list-lower-nested-rawcase`). A nested list opener is interpreted by its OWN case, independently of the outer opener's case. A lowercase nested `[[ul]]`/`[[ol]]` is a genuine list: when the outer opener is raw-case (which never pairs), the outer list closes after the current item, the nested lowercase list is emitted as the next sibling list, and the remaining outer closer run stays literal. A raw-case nested `[[UL]]`/`[[OL]]` is an item boundary: its opener and closer are consumed and the following `[[LI]]` becomes a sibling item in the SAME list. REFCORR8 and REFCORR9 normalized only two of the four (outer,nested) case combinations; R13-S1 adds the two missing symmetric siblings as two pre-normalization rewrites in `List.php`. This is one rule with two case branches, not two new special cases, because the deciding predicate is the nested tag's case in both branches.

R13-S2 (residual-whitespace strip after the nested-closer strip, fixes C-R5F-P1-4 and C-R5F-P1-5). After the leading run of `[[/li]]`/`[[/ul|ol]]` closers is consumed from the literal tail, the whitespace run immediately following the last stripped closer is consumed with it. That LF is the stripped closer's own line end and is already accounted for by the emitted item wire; leaving it behind makes the reference emit one extra Newline witness `<br />` after `in` where live emits none. The PHP closer-strip regex gains one trailing `\s*`; the mirror's `stripLeadingClosers12` already drops leading whitespace after a stripped closer, so this clause is PHP-only by inspection.

R13-S3 (whole-tail footnoteblock hoist with preserved LF witness, fixes C-R5F-P1-2, C-R5F-P1-3 and C-R5F-S2-2). The post-failed-closer tail is scanned for the first `[[footnoteblock]]` marker by token identity (literal-marker fallback) over the WHOLE tail, not only the bytes after the surviving final block closer. The tail is split at that marker: everything before it (the final closer and any trailing prose) remains in the absorbing item, and the marker plus everything after it is emitted after the list-end token. The final-closer split and the leading-closer strip apply to the pre-marker bytes only. The pre-marker bytes are NOT rtrimmed: their trailing LF is the Newline witness that live renders as `<br />`. This single symmetric rule covers both a marker after the final closer (P1-2/-3) and a marker before it (S2-2), which the prior clause treated differently.
