# R5B-R1 pre-probe ledger — RENDER-SIDE BLOCK AND CONTAINER RULES (round 5b)

Post-REFCORR8 canonical ground (ref-php commit b909f33, ftml mirror with SpecRefcorr8 pins, build green). This ledger was written in full before any live probe. Every suspicion below was raised by fresh static reading of the corrected Xhtml render sources under ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/ (Div, Span, Size, Collapsible, Tabview, List, Blockquote, Clearfloat, Note, Center) and their parse-side companions, plus local reference-harness renders of the same sources (php is not a live probe). Live Wikidot behavior is definitional however malformed. Evidence order: ftml corpus and integration pins, frozen parity captures, then fresh live sandbox probes fetched once and cached. The decisive live byte for each row is its CONFIRM condition; the byte that clears it is its CLEAR condition. All rows are graded only after the standard probe norm() mask plus the campaign frame mask, so inter-tag newlines, trailing paragraph newlines, &nbsp;/U+00A0 spelling, random tab ids, date attributes, attribute ordering, token-id counter digits and Stage 2 purifyHtml frame differences are already normalized away and cannot by themselves confirm a divergence. Stage 2 frame differences are CLEARED by rule, never confirmed. A round-5 cleared shape is re-probed here only where the mechanism changed in REFCORR8; those rows carry a Mechanism reason below. No suspicion may be added after probing without its own new ledger row and probe.
