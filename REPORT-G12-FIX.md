## The repaired discipline, per type

Items now carry line-start information: an opener is `openTag b ls`, a closer is `close b ls s` with `ls` the line-start flag and `s` the suffix (the text after the closer token on its line). A closer is accepted when both per-type gates pass (`accepts`): the suffix gate is blank-headed (empty, or first character a space or tab, arbitrary text after that blank) for code and math, any suffix at all for footnote and collapsible, and all-blank for bibliography; the position gate requires a line-start closer only for math and is vacuous for the other four types. Collapsible, footnote and bibliography keep the first-closer scan (`scanFirst`): the first accepted closer ends the block and nested openers are literal body text. Code and math use the new backtracking recursive scan (`scanRec`, fuel-bounded as `scanRecF` in the house style of CodeRule, fuel supplied as stream length + 1, which the tail theorem's call structure never exhausts): at a line-start same-type opener the nested block is attempted first, and the block is used only when it is not rejected and the continuation scan of its rest also succeeds; otherwise the opener falls back to literal body text and the scan continues on the tail. The one rejection is math's adjacent-closer lookahead (`rejectsNested`): a nested math block whose closer has an all-blank suffix and whose rest begins with a math closer does not count as a nested block. An accepted inner closer is recorded in the body with its suffix erased exactly when the suffix is blank (the pre-existing `eraseBlankItem` normalisation, now position-preserving); a rejected closer is recorded with its suffix, as literal body text. This reproduces every measured behaviour in the brief: the unbalanced two-opener one-closer shapes form one block ending at the first closer with the inner opener literal (the nested attempt succeeds but its continuation fails, so the fallback fires), a mid-line inner opener never nests, math-adjclose closes at the first closer with the second literal, a mid-line math closer is literal while a mid-line code closer is accepted, and the recorded profiles survive (next section).
