Mechanism. Live tolerates, immediately before a leading `_default:` category, a maximal run of three kinds of lead material: Unicode White_Space characters, the U+FEFF BOM, and the `*` new-tab marker. The single general predicate is therefore `lead = (White_Space | U+FEFF | U+002A)*`, matched case-insensitively and anchored, with the whole matched run stripped as part of the category prefix. The Unicode White_Space property is {U+0009..U+000D, U+0020, U+0085, U+00A0, U+1680, U+2000..U+200A, U+2028, U+2029, U+202F, U+205F, U+3000}; BOM is not White_Space in current Unicode and star is a marker, so both are added explicitly. Every byte shape cached live — 09, 0B, 0C, 20, C2 A0 (U+00A0), E3 80 80 (U+3000), E2 80 A8 (U+2028), E2 80 A9 (U+2029), E2 80 83 (U+2003), E2 80 89 (U+2009), E2 80 AF (U+202F), EF BB BF (U+FEFF) and 2A, including the eight-byte mixed run R5CP2-05 and the double star R5CP2-07 — is a member of this one class; byte-zero NUL is not. The PHP implements the class as the byte-oriented pattern `/^(?:[\x09\x0A\x0B\x0C\x0D\x20\x2A]|\xC2[\x85\xA0]|\xE1\x9A\x80|\xE2\x80[\x80-\x8A\xA8\xA9\xAF]|\xE2\x81\x9F|\xE3\x80\x80|\xEF\xBB\xBF)*_default:/i`, a mechanical UTF-8 encoding of the code-point set. The code-point form `/^[\p{White_Space}\x{FEFF}\x{2A}]*_default:/iu` is principled and was measured in scratch/pcre-probe.php to match the same shapes, but with `/u` preg_match returns false on a malformed UTF-8 tail (demonstrated on `"\xC2\xA0_default:x\xFF"`), silently disabling the strip; REFCORR8 rejected `/u` for exactly that reason, so this correction keeps the byte-oriented invariant and records the `/u` alternative as rejected. The separate single-star ordering rule on `$slugSource` in REFCORR9 is removed because `*` is now a member of the single lead run; the URL-branch star at lines 154-158, the display-text path that reads `$raw`, the anchored strip before transliteration, and `strlen($defPrefix[0])` byte accounting are unchanged. Limitation: adopting a principled Unicode class admits unevidenced members — 0x0A, 0x0D, U+0085 (C2 85), U+1680 (E1 9A 80), U+2000..U+2002 and U+2004..U+2008 and U+200A (E2 80 80..82 and 84..8A), U+205F (E2 81 9F), and star-after-whitespace combinations — that have no cached live value (0x0A/0x0D/NUL are also unstorable in the sandbox). These are general-rule consequences, not live-confirmed wins; byte-zero NUL, non-leading `_default:`, generic colon prefixes, doubled prefixes, starred pages/URLs and all post-REFCORR6 ASCII/transliteration shapes remain excluded or unchanged, as the before/after table shows.
