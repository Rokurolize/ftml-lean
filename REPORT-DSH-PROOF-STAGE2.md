This report closes the Stage 2 PROOF gap opened by BRIEF-DSH-PROOF-STAGE2.md. The conformance matter was already closed in the coordinator tree (266 cases, 257 agreeing outright, 9 classified harness-frame, 0 open); this package addresses the 21 `partial def`s in the mirror's Stage 2 model and the Tier 3 contract of ADVISOR-PROOF-POLICY.md. ## 1. What the partial definitions were and how each was totalized

The source `stage2/stage2-model/Stage2.lean` (1,210 LOC) had 21 `partial def`s: six lexical/tag scanners (`scanQuoted`, `scanTagRest`, `parseAndNormAttrs`, `scanUntilCloseTag`, `pass1Loop`, `tokenizeHtmlLoop`), four T6 paragraph-unwrap scanners (`containsUnwrapTrigger`, `unwrapScopeScan`, `findFirstCloseP`, `pass2Loop`), four stack pops (`closeInlinesForBlockEnd`, `popDepthPopInline`, `popDepthNoIstack`, `popDepthDeferIstack`, plus `popCrossedForReopen`), one repair loop (`repairInlineEnd`), and five tree/serialization passes (`unwindTreeFrames`, `processNodePass4`, `serializeTreeRaw`, `serializeChildrenPass5`, `serializeNodePass5`). Every one is now total, with zero `partial` keywords in `LeanFtml/Proof/Stage2TidyCore.lean` or `Stage2FrameComp.lean`.

The totalization device is explicit in each type: scanners take a leading `fuel : Nat` with a safe default of `cs.length + 1` and a total fallback at fuel 0 (`none`, `acc.reverse`, or the accumulated string); stack pops recurse structurally on the pre-existing `count : Nat` or `openStack.length`; `repairInlineEnd` recurses on its existing `fuel`; tree passes recurse structurally on `HtmlNode`/`List HtmlNode` with a `height + 1` default. The pass-5 mutual pair needs `2 * height + 1` (node) and `2 * nodesHeight + 2` (children) because a Node to Children to Node descent spends two fuel units per tree level — an earlier `height + 1` and then `height + 2` default silently dropped leaf text, and that defect was found and corrected before delivery. Each original name and arity is preserved by a wrapper; a `T`-suffixed twin exposes the raw fueled definition for induction. The full 21-row old-name to new-total-def mapping with the device used per definition is in `proof-stage2-artifacts/totalization-notes.md`.

Decoupling: T6 (`pass2ParagraphUnwrap` and its helpers) and T11 (`dropBlockSandwichedBrs`) are quarantined in `LeanFtml/Proof/Stage2FrameComp.lean`. `Stage2TidyCore` imports that module but applies those passes only when `Stage2Config.applyFrameComp = true`; `stage2TidyCore cfg html` is the strict libtidy core (passes 1, 3, 4-core, 5) and `transformTotal html` is the byte-faithful full port of `Stage2.transform`. `LeanFtml/Proof/Stage2.lean` is the adoption umbrella. Fidelity evidence: a pass-by-pass differential against the unmodified original model gave `corpus=1557 mismatches=0`, and the exact 266 settled battery inputs gave `cases=266 portVOriginal=0 portVRecorded=0 origVRecorded=0`; an independent root harness (`proof-stage2-integration/`) added 2,387 more cases with 0 mismatches.

## 2. What is now proved

`LeanFtml/Proof/Stage2Proof.lean` delivers 26 kernel-checked theorems about the totalized definitions, organized in three tiers.

Unconditional structural theorems: `stage2_well_formed` proves `WFHtmlNode (parseWellFormedTree html)` for every `html`, by structural induction through `wf_addNodeToStack`, `wf_rootFrame`, `stage2TreeStep_wf`, `stage2_fold_frames_wf`, `wf_unwindTreeFramesT` and `wf_unwindTreeFrames`; `parseWellFormedTree_eq` ties the fueled parser to the public name. Repair-state invariants are unconditional for the relevant operations: `pushInline_isPushed`, `pushInline_istack_length`, `popDepthNoIstack_savedIstacks`, `inScopeDepth_cell_top_blocks`, and the T5 table-cell scope-wall facts `stage2_t5_td_enter_clears_istack`, `stage2_t5_td_enter_saves_istack`, `stage2_t5_td_exit_restores_istack`. `stage2_parametric_in_text` proves that `stepToken` does not depend on text payload bytes beyond emptiness and leading-whitespace class (`SameTextFrame`), which is the Lean-side justification for the finite skeleton quotient.

Repair laws are proved as universal statements over explicitly described repair-state shapes: `stage2_t1_replaceNbsp_single` (T1), `stage2_t3_anchor_bound` and `stage2_t3_single_crossing_rotation` (T3), `stage2_t4_multi_crossing_keeps_target_open` (T4), `stage2_t7_block_start_closes_p` (T7), `stage2_t9_hoist_empty_prunable` (T9), and `stage2_t12_stray_block_closer` plus `stage2_t12_stray_inline_closer_empty_stack` (T12). Each theorem quantifies over the whole repair state, so it is universal within its stated configuration; the configuration and the fuel witness (`2` for T3/T4) are explicit in the theorem type rather than hidden.

The two contract laws that cannot be stated without a domain predicate are stated honestly with one. `stage2_wf_identity` proves `stage2TidyCore cfg s = blockLayoutOnly cfg s` under `NormalizedWF cfg s`; `stage2_idempotent` proves `stage2TidyCore cfg (stage2TidyCore cfg s) = stage2TidyCore cfg s` under `CoreImageCanonical cfg (stage2TidyCore cfg s)`, via `stage2TidyCore_eq_of_pass_fixed`. The named remainder is exactly the missing antecedent: proving that every Stage-1 output satisfies `NormalizedWF`, and that every core image satisfies `CoreImageCanonical`, requires string-level fixed-point theorems for Tidy's frame/tag, repair and whitespace passes; those are differential facts about external C scanners and are certified by the bounded record, not forced here. Full-universal string-level T3/T4 laws also need a fuel-monotonicity lemma for `repairInlineEnd`, and T2/T8/T10 have no separate universal string-level theorem in this package; they are covered by the structural invariants and the conformance record. No axiom, `sorry`, `native_decide` or `bv_decide` was introduced to close any of these gaps.

## 3. The bounded oracle certificate

### Certificate domain and generator

The TCB-5 domain is the parametricity-quotiented tag-skeleton language at the policy's bound: depth at most 4, length at most 8, over 12 tag classes (`span`, `strong`, `em`, `a`, `div`, `p`, `table`, `td`, `ul`, `li`, `br`, `code`) plus a separate text terminal `x`; justification for each class is in `proof-stage2-certificate/README.md`. The bound is the smallest one exposing T4 multi-level crossings: a k-element crossing needs k+1 matched pairs, so k=2 needs 6 tokens/depth 3 and the documented three-element crossing (p15/q04) needs 8 tokens/depth 4. The generator `proof-stage2-certificate/generate.py` enumerates exhaustively (depth-first over tokens, open stack, used-class mask and leaf flag, no sampling), deduplicates, and sorts by UTF-8 byte length then bytes.

### Recorded cardinality, hashes and result

Exact cardinality: 1,058,060 canonical skeletons. SHA-256 of the canonical sorted corpus: `0c01780e3ac0b30a1984e9bb5b1969a70ce7ec04a859ab0a043aebce62c64281`. SHA-256 of the per-case record `certificate.jsonl`: `88f4920b2d491cf90c92885bbbddac3d6beb5ddfe91b05c0b82546034ff8449f`. The exhaustive differential compared `Stage2TidyCore.transformTotal` (via `lean-ftml-stage2-cli --framed`) against the local libtidy 5.8.0 oracle (`oracle_batch.c`, a byte-for-byte reproduction of `conformance-tidy-oracle/tidy_oracle.c`'s wrapper, options, two `tidySaveBuffer` calls and PHP extraction), byte-for-byte after the conformance record's outer-whitespace strip. Result: 35,050 agreements and 1,023,010 divergences. The zero-gap condition is therefore NOT met; `proof-stage2-certificate/manifest.json` records 10 minimal witnesses. Three of them: `<a></a>` gives model `<a></a>` but oracle empty; `<li></li>` gives model `<li></li>` but oracle `<ul>` wrapping; `<td></td>` gives model bare `<td>` but oracle implicit `<table><tr><td></td></tr></table>`. I independently re-probed `tidy_oracle` on these shapes and confirmed the divergences are real, not a driver artifact. No class or case was excluded to hide a divergence and the model was not modified in response; per the brief, fixes belong to the coordinator lane and this line is stopped here. The divergences are dominated by libtidy implicit structural repair on bare fragments that Stage 1 would not emit (bare `li`/`td` wrapping, empty `a`/`ul`/`div`/`table` pruning), with empty inline classes a further group.

### Honest maximum for the external boundary

A full functional equality `Stage2TidyCore = libtidy.so.58` is not a Lean theorem: libtidy is roughly 50,000 lines of external C and is not a Lean term. The certificate plus the 266-case conformance record is the honest maximum for this boundary, and it is reported as such. Above the bound sit unbounded global rescans and cascading multi-level crossings; the named Stage 2 remainders U1 (p15 tail after a dropped span), U2 (paragraph-unwrap boundary), U3 (break-drop bounds), U4 (anchor boundary beyond depth 1) and U5 (lone image/hr paragraphs) from the coordinator's `stage2/REPORT-STAGE2.md` sit outside this certificate domain and are not claimed. The Lean module `LeanFtml/Proof/Stage2Certificate.lean` records the alphabet, bounds, cardinality and hashes as axiom-free definitions and finite theorems; it deliberately does not assert the external differential as a Lean theorem.

## 5. What remains and why

### Proof remainder

Universal string-level fixed-point theorems for passes 1–3 are the missing antecedents of `stage2_wf_identity` and `stage2_idempotent`; those are differential facts about Tidy's external C scanners and are certified by the conformance record rather than forced in Lean. Lifting the T3/T4/T7 concrete fuel witnesses (2) and concrete repair-state shapes to every sufficient fuel needs a `repairInlineEnd` fuel-monotonicity lemma; T2, T8 and T10 have no separate universal string/tree-level theorem in this package, and full string commutation for `stage2_parametric_in_text` needs a tokenization-respects-substitution lemma. Each of these is named rather than papered over. None was discharged with an axiom.

### Certificate remainder and coordinator actions

The 1,023,010 divergences are the material open item. Ten minimal witnesses are in `proof-stage2-certificate/manifest.json`; the dominant families are libtidy implicit structural repair on bare fragments (bare `li` to `ul`, bare `td` to `table/tr`) and pruning of empty `a`/`ul`/`div`/`table`, with empty inline classes a further group. Per the brief, the model was not modified and no class or case was excluded; model repairs belong to the coordinator lane. Beyond the bound sit unbounded rescans and cascading crossings, the full 50k-LOC libtidy behavior, and the named U1–U5 remainders of the coordinator's Stage 2 record. Source-side replacement of `stage2/stage2-model/Stage2.lean` is likewise coordinator adoption work: this package adds the totalized modules under `lean-ftml/LeanFtml/Proof/`, with the old-to-new mapping in `totalization-notes.md`, and the coordinator wires the import. The unrelated pre-existing `ProofNotesFootnote` whole-build failure is recorded above and is not a Stage 2 action.
