## 2. C-STAGE2-1 — Div attribute order: SETTLED, reference corrected

Live's rule, from the eight permutation probes diva-a1 to a8 (all valid, stable, source-matching): a div's attributes are emitted in the fixed order id, class, style, role, type, followed by the data-* attributes in authored order. The order is invariant under authored permutation (a1 to a4 serve identically), the five-name ranking is fixed by a5 and a7, and the data-* family keeps authored relative order without internal sorting (a6: data-z before data-a, as authored). This contradicts the reference's name sort on three independent points, and it explains the frozen p34 capture (live id before class) that raised the candidate.

The correction is in ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Div.php: the `ksort($args)` is replaced by an explicit construction of the emission order. It is realized at parse time, not in the renderer as the brief's initial framing suggested, because the parse's sort is the point where the data-* authored order is destroyed and no renderer-side reorder could recover it (probe a6 is the discriminator); the renderer is unchanged for this candidate and still emits the stored order.
