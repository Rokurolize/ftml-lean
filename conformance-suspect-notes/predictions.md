S1 (three-paragraph note). Construct: Parse/Default/Footnoteblock.php:68-80, `$marked` flag in the `/\n[ ]*\n/` callback; only the first paragraph break gets a Footnoteitem `parabreak` token (ADOPT-CORR fnb24). Why suspicious: the comment says "only the first break is marked" because later paragraphs supposedly separate through the paragraph-end renderer; a one-off flag is exactly the kind of asymmetric guard that can be wrong on a third paragraph. Prediction A (code right): source `x[[footnote]]p1\n\np2\n\np3[[/footnote]]` renders p1, a preserved break, then p2 and p3 joined in one block (second break deleted by Tighten). Prediction B (skeptic): all three paragraphs are separately rendered. Probe `n3para`.

S2 (tab-only blank line in a note). Construct: same `/\n[ ]*\n/` pattern; `[ ]*` admits spaces only, not tabs, while Prefilter's whitespace-only-line trim admits `\s`. Why suspicious: a tab-only blank line is whitespace to the earlier trim but invisible to the parabreak marker. Prediction A (code right): `x[[footnote]]p1\n\t\np2[[/footnote]]` loses the break (p1 and p2 joined). Prediction B (skeptic): any whitespace-only line still yields a break. Probe `n-tab-break`.

S3 (blank residual line does not reset the walk state). Construct: Parse/Default/Bibliography.php:72-73, `if (trim($text) === '') return '';` leaves `$prev` untouched, so a blank line between two residual lines does not break a residual run. Why suspicious: state carried between lines with no reset on the empty-line case. Prediction A (code right): `foo\n\nbar` inside a bibliography body joins as `foo bar` (one space); Prediction B (skeptic): the blank line yields a line break or separate paragraphs. Probe `bib-res-blank`.

S4 (indented bibliography opener). Construct: Parse/Default/Bibliography.php:36, `'/^\[\[bibliography.../m'` with `^` at line start. Why suspicious: the earlier R2 battery pinned `[[ bibliography]]` (space inside brackets) and a trailing-tab closer, but not indentation before the opener. Prediction A (code right): a single leading space makes the whole block literal and `: a : A` becomes a definition list. Prediction B (skeptic): the block still forms. Probe `bib-indent`.

S5 (lazy closer that does not end a line). Prediction A (code right): `[[bibliography]]\n: a : A\n[[/bibliography]] tail` with no later closer stays literal (and a following bibcite errors). Prediction B (skeptic): the block forms at the first closer, tail rendered outside. Probe `bib-close-tail`.
