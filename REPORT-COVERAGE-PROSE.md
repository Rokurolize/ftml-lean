The formalization is in the new `lean-ftml/LeanFtml/CoverageProse.lean`, wired into `LeanFtml.lean`. Its `ProseClass` is an inductive enumeration of 53 branch classes, one for every branch the thirteen rules can take on authored source (`allClasses_length` proves the count is 53, `allClasses_nodup` proves the enumeration has no duplicates). The input side is `ProseShape`, a disjoint sum of thirteen decision types over abstract source features, never over raw characters: run lengths for the `{4,}` rules, a side-marker variant for Clearfloat, a boolean per transformation for the sequence rules, and a constructor per exclusive outcome for the rest. `classify` maps each shape to the LIST of branch classes its decision structure enables, because Prefilter, Tighten and Typography are fixed sequences of independent transformations and can fire several branches on one source, while the other ten rules have mutually exclusive outcomes and return singletons. The branch conditions themselves are formalized: `hzBranches` and `spBranches` encode the `{4,}` threshold with theorems `hzFire_of_count`, `hzLiteral_of_count`, `spFire_of_count`, `spLiteral_of_count`; `cfBranches` encodes the single-side group with `cfDoubleSide_of_dashes` and `cfBoth_of_dashes`; `tnBranches` derives the second whitespace fold from a color edge-space move; `tyBranches` derives the no-pattern class from the nine typography patterns by negation. The two branches that cannot fire on authored source are proved dead rather than classed: Delimiter's search for the internal 0xFF byte (`delimiter_dead_branch_has_no_author_shape`, backed by `FrameRules.delimiterRule_id` and the UTF-8 argument) and Typography's `---` statement, which the earlier `--` replacement consumes (`typography_dash3_statement_dead`, probe `typo-dash3`). The bare `$` money alternative is the same kind of dead PCRE anchor and is recorded in the Typography branch provenance, not counted as a class.
