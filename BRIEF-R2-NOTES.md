# Brief: R2 footnote and bibliography family stage as a TEAM lane (DSH)

You are the root session of a team lane in the ftml Lean equivalence loop's reference-PHP workstream (R2). The loop is building a PHP reference implementation that reproduces live Wikidot behavior: the fossil Text_Wiki snapshot, behavior-corrected rule by rule against live evidence (the fossil is a skeleton, never evidence; live behavior wins every disagreement). Earlier stages are complete and published as snapshots of the public repo; their catalogs are conformance-refphp/R2-INCLUDE.md, R2-FRAME.md, R2-CODE.md, R2-FORM-RAW.md, R2-MODULE.md, R2-NEXT.md, and R2-LINK.md in this worktree, and they are the shape to imitate. This stage covers the six rules nineteenth to twenty-fourth in pipeline order, the footnote and bibliography family: Footnote, Footnoteitem, Footnoteblock, Bibitem, Bibliography, Bibcite. This codebase is yours: what it's for and how it's built are your call, and you'll keep building on it.

This family's defining property is shared document state: footnotes are numbered and collected across the whole document and rendered where the footnoteblock sits, and bibliography entries are keyed, ordered, and resolved by citations that can appear before or after the bibliography. The per-rule differentials therefore only mean anything inside whole documents, and the root's integration work is the center of this stage, not an afterthought. This stage runs as a team with that in mind. Your FIRST deliverable, before spawning any teammate, is the catalog skeleton: create conformance-refphp/R2-NOTES.md with the stage's method paragraph, one empty headed part per rule, an empty interactions part, an empty findings list, and a final line reading BLOCKED: integration pending (the stage watcher treats that exact line as work-in-progress, not a terminal marker). Fill it in as sections land, and replace the final line only at true completion. After the skeleton exists, create six tasks on the shared task board, one per rule, spawn one teammate per task with the per-rule brief below scoped to its rule, and coordinate. You own the shared infrastructure, the document-state seams, the cross-rule interactions, and the catalog; you do not do a teammate's rule work yourself except where a rule is left unfinished and you say so in the catalog. Write prose in files as flowing paragraphs on single lines, never hard-wrapped at a fixed column.
