## The two-stage fact this mission exists to formalize

Live Wikidot renders in two stages (owner's consult ruling, 2026-11-04, and the standing premise of the whole equivalence programme). Stage 1 is Text_Wiki: it pairs constructs in the source text and emits HTML; the live-validated PHP reference in ref-php/ is the programme's Stage 1 spec, and its Lean mirror is complete for the parse phase. Stage 2 is WikiTransformation::purifyHtml, a libtidy-based repair pass over Stage 1's HTML: it re-nests the markup with an HTML-repair stack in which a closing tag closes the innermost open element **with the same HTML tag name**, swapping closers where distinct source constructs render as the same tag (span, size, color, and strikethrough all render as <span>, so their closers interchange). Stage 2 is also where the space-run collapse and the paragraph-unwrap of block content live (settled at the R2 PARA stage), and it is the layer that has absorbed, as unexplained "frame surface", a long tail of divergences across the R2 stages: the INLINE2 crossing matrix (63 interaction probes whose split was dominated by crossing shapes), the CONTAIN and LIST interaction crossings, and the block-joining surface recorded as F-R2-58 surface A in GAP-LEDGER.md (in this worktree).
